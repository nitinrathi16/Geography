🧊 Origin — Exact Source

Primary source (Bhagirathi): Gangotri Glacier snout Gomukh (Cow's Mouth), alt. 3892m, Uttarkashi district, Uttarakhand. Bhagirathi flows south through a gorge.

Primary source (Alakhnanda): Satopanth Glacier, Chamoli district, Uttarakhand. Alakhnanda is the larger headstream by volume.

Confluence (Ganga is born): At Dev Prayag (470m), Tehri Garhwal district, where Bhagirathi + Alakhnanda merge → becomes GANGA. Dev Prayag is the southernmost Panch Prayag.

🔑 UPPSC Trick: Bhagirathi is the cultural/official source; Alakhnanda has more volume. Ganga only officially begins at Dev Prayag.

🗺️ UP Angle — Ganga in Uttar Pradesh
Ganga enters UP at Haridwar-Bijnor boundary and exits at Ghazipur-Ballia (Bihar border). It flows ~1000 km through UP.
UP's main rivers are Ganga tributaries: Yamuna (right), Ram Ganga, Gomti, Ghaghra, Sharda, Gandak — all join Ganga system within or near UP.
Upper Ganga Canal (Haridwar to Kanpur, 1939 km total system) — one of the longest canals in Asia. Irrigates Doab region (western UP).
National Waterway NW-1 (Allahabad/Prayagraj → Haldia, 1620 km) passes through Varanasi, Ghazipur, Patna. Crucial for inland navigation.
Namami Gange Mission — 97 of 97 targeted UP sewage treatment projects have been initiated. UP has the most towns on Ganga (28+ towns).
Rihand Dam (Govind Ballabh Pant Sagar) in Sonbhadra, UP — largest man-made lake in India by area; on Rihand River (Son tributary).

🧊 Origin — Yamunotri

Glacier: Champasar Glacier (also called Yamunotri Glacier) near Banderpunch massif, at 6387m altitude, Uttarkashi district, Uttarakhand.

🗺️ UP Angle — Yamuna in Uttar Pradesh
Yamuna enters UP near Faridabad-Mathura boundary and flows through Mathura, Agra, Etawah, Hamirpur, Banda until joining Ganga at Prayagraj.
Major UP right-bank tributaries of Yamuna: Chambal (joins near Etawah), Betwa (Hamirpur), Ken (Banda) — all from MP/Vindhyas.
Ken-Betwa River Interlinking Project (2021): Daudhan Dam on Ken river (Panna, MP) → transfers water to Betwa → irrigates water-scarce Bundelkhand region of UP (Jhansi, Banda, Hamirpur, Lalitpur, Mahoba, Chitrakoot). First interlinking project in India.
Western Yamuna Canal starts from Tajewala Barrage, supplies water to Delhi, Haryana, and UP's Saharanpur-Muzaffarnagar districts.
Betwa River flows through UP's Jhansi, Lalitpur, Hamirpur (Bundelkhand) — critical for this drought-prone region's water security.

🧊 Origin — Angsi Glacier, Tibet

Glacier: Angsi Glacier (Chemayungdung Glacier), near Mansarovar Lake, Burang County, Tibet (China). Altitude: ~5150m.

In Tibet: Called Yarlung Tsangpo / Tsangpo. Flows EASTWARD for ~1600 km at 4000m+ altitude — world's highest major river. Makes a dramatic U-turn (hairpin bend) at Namcha Barwa peak (7782m) creating the world's deepest gorge.

In Arunachal Pradesh: Called Dihang / Siang. Joins with Lohit and Dibang at Sadiya → becomes Brahmaputra.

In Bangladesh: Called Jamuna. Joins Ganga (Padma) + Meghna → drains into Bay of Bengal.

🔑 UPPSC 2020 Q52: Brahmaputra is a Trans-Himalayan river (along with Indus and Sutlej). These rivers are antecedent to the Himalayas — older than the mountains!

🌊 Ghaghra (Saryu / Karnali) — UP's Largest River

🧊 Origin

Glacier: Mapchachungo Glacier, Humla district, Nepal. Also draws from Karnali (Nepal). Enters India near Bahraich (UP) as Ghaghra. The portion flowing past Ayodhya is called Saryu. In Nepal it is Karnali.

📏
Length: ~1080 km (longest left-bank tributary of Ganga)
🛕
Ayodhya: Sri Ram Janmabhoomi is on the Saryu river banks
🏗️
Girija Barrage: Bahraich, UP — on Sharda river (meets Ghaghra here)
🔁
Meets Ganga: Near Saidpur/Dorighat, Ballia/Chhapra area (UP-Bihar border)

Key UP districts: Bahraich → Gonda → Faizabad (Ayodhya) → Basti → Gorakhpur area → Deoria → meets Ganga near Saidpur

Length: 960 km. Meets Ganga at Saidpur, Ghazipur district, UP.

🏙️

Q1 · UPPSC 2023 (Direct Pattern)
Which one of the following is the correct confluence point of rivers Alakhnanda and Bhagirathi?
A Vishnu Prayag
B Karn Prayag
C Rudra Prayag
D Dev Prayag
✅ Dev Prayag (d) — At Dev Prayag (470m), Tehri Garhwal district, Uttarakhand, Bhagirathi and Alakhnanda merge to form the Ganga. This is the southernmost Panch Prayag. Vishnu Prayag = Dhauliganga+Alakhnanda; Karn Prayag = Pindar+Alakhnanda; Rudra Prayag = Mandakini+Alakhnanda. This exact question appeared in UPPSC Prelims 2023!
Q2 · UPPSC 2023 (Direct Pattern)
Consider the following statements: (1) Ram Ganga river joins the Ganga at Kannauj. (2) River Betwa joins the Yamuna near Prayagraj. Which is/are correct?
A Only 1
B Only 2
C Both 1 and 2
D Neither 1 nor 2
✅ Only 1 (a) — Statement 1: Ram Ganga joins Ganga near Kannauj (UP) ✔. Statement 2: Betwa joins Yamuna at Hamirpur (UP), NOT near Prayagraj ✗. Betwa meets Yamuna about 200 km upstream of Prayagraj. From UPPSC 2023!
Q3 · UPPSC 2020 (Direct Pattern)
Consider the following tributaries of River Yamuna and arrange them from West to East: 1. Betwa 2. Ken 3. Sindh 4. Chambal
A 4, 3, 1 and 2
B 1, 2, 3 and 4
C 3, 2, 1 and 4
D 2, 3, 1 and 4
✅ (a) 4,3,1,2 = Chambal → Sindh → Betwa → Ken — From West to East: Chambal joins near Etawah (most western), Sindh joins near Lalitpur-Gwalior area, Betwa joins at Hamirpur, Ken joins near Banda (closest to Prayagraj). All are RIGHT bank peninsular tributaries. Direct from UPPSC 2020 Q68!
Q4 · UPPSC 2021 (Direct)
Which one of the following rivers is NOT part of the Indian Ganga river basin?
A Punpun river
B Ajoy river
C Jalangi river
D Jonk river
✅ Jonk river (d) — Jonk River is a tributary of the Mahanadi (Chhattisgarh), NOT the Ganga basin. Punpun joins Ganga near Fatwa (Bihar). Ajoy and Jalangi are in WB and drain into Bhagirathi/Hooghly (part of Ganga system). Directly from UPPSC 2021 Q5!
Q5 · UPPSC 2021 (Assertion-Reason)
Assertion (A): Teesta river was earlier a tributary of Ganga, now it is a tributary of Brahmaputra. Reason (R): River capturing is a major feature of Himalayan rivers.
A Both A & R true; R is correct explanation of A
B Both A & R true; R is NOT correct explanation of A
C A is true but R is false
D A is false but R is true
✅ (a) Both A & R true; R is correct explanation — The Teesta DID flow into the Ganga in the past but was "captured" (pirated) by the Brahmaputra — a classic example of river capture in the Himalayas. River capture (piracy) is indeed a major Himalayan river feature due to young, steep, actively eroding rivers. Directly from UPPSC 2021 Q63!
Q6 · UPPSC 2022 (Direct)
The main purpose of 'Ganga Action Plan' project launched in 1985 was —
A Make its water pollution-free only
B Utilize river water for irrigation only
C Build new dams over Ganga river valley
D None of the above
✅ None of the above (d) — Ganga Action Plan (1985) aimed at: (i) reducing pollution load, (ii) intercepting and diverting domestic sewage, (iii) preventing direct cremation on banks, (iv) afforestation. It was NOT limited to just one purpose, so "None of the above" (considering options a, b, c are partial). This was directly asked in UPPSC 2022 Q121!
Q7 · UPPSC 2025 (New Pattern)
Which of the following pairs of (Dam — River) is NOT correctly matched?
A Baglihar Dam — Chenab
B Pandoh Dam — Ravi
C Srisailam Dam — Tungabhadra
D Both B and C
✅ Both B and C (d) — Pandoh Dam is on the Beas river (HP), NOT Ravi. Srisailam Dam is on the Krishna river (AP/Telangana), NOT Tungabhadra (Tungabhadra Dam is at Hospet, Karnataka). Baglihar Dam IS on Chenab (Ramban, J&K) ✔. Asked in UPPSC 2025 Q92!
Q8 · UP Special — Gomti Origin
Gomti river, which flows through Lucknow, originates from which of the following?
A Milam Glacier, Pithoragarh
B Gomat Taal, Pilibhit (UP)
C Doodhatoli Range, Chamoli
D Yamunotri Glacier, Uttarkashi
✅ Gomat Taal, Pilibhit (b) — Gomti originates from Gomat Taal (also called Fulhar Jheel) in Pilibhit district, UP. It is the ONLY major river born entirely in the Terai plains of UP (not from mountains/glaciers). Milam Glacier → Sharda river. Doodhatoli → Ram Ganga. Yamunotri → Yamuna. A high-priority UP-special question!
Q9 · UPPSC 2020 (Trans-Himalayan Rivers)
Which of the following is a Trans-Himalayan river?
A Jhelum
B Sutlej
C Ganga
D Ravi
✅ Sutlej (b) — Trans-Himalayan rivers originate north of the Himalayas (in Tibet/Tibetan Plateau) and cut through the mountains. The three Trans-Himalayan rivers: Indus, Sutlej, Brahmaputra. Sutlej originates from Rakas Tal (near Mansarovar), Tibet. Jhelum and Ravi originate in Indian/Kashmir Himalayas. Ganga originates at Gangotri (south side of Himalayas). Direct from UPPSC 2020!
Q10 · UPPSC Special — Tehri Dam
Tehri Dam, the tallest dam in India (261 m), is built on which river?
A Alakhnanda + Bhilangana
B Bhagirathi + Bhilangana
C Mandakini + Bhagirathi
D Pindar + Alakhnanda
✅ Bhagirathi + Bhilangana (b) — Tehri Dam is built at the confluence of Bhagirathi and its tributary Bhilangana, near Tehri town, Uttarakhand. It is India's tallest dam at 261m and generates 2400 MW of power. Tehri Hydro Development Corporation (THDC) manages it. Key fact: Tehri town was submerged when the reservoir was filled. Bhilangana flows from Khatling Glacier.
