📌
Mnemonic — Cold Currents on WEST coasts: Labrador (NE Canada), Canary (NW Africa), Benguela (SW Africa), Humboldt (W S.America), California (W USA), W Australian. Warm on EAST coasts: Gulf Stream (E USA), Kuroshio (E Japan), Brazilian (E Brazil), Agulhas (E Africa).
🌊
El Niño Impact on India: El Niño → warm Pacific waters → weak Indian Monsoon → drought. La Niña → cool Pacific → strong Monsoon → floods. ENSO cycle affects India's agriculture critically.
