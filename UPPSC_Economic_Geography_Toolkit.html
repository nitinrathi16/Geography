Wheat Belt — North-West India + UP

EXAM HOT

🌾 Core Wheat States

  • Punjab — Granary of India; Amritsar, Ludhiana districts
  • Haryana — Karnal, Kurukshetra (Green Revolution hub)
  • UP (Meerut, Agra, Bareilly) — Western UP wheat zone
  • Rajasthan — Ganganagar, Hanumangarh (Indira Gandhi Canal)
  • MP (Madhya Pradesh) — Malwa Plateau (Ujjain, Indore)

📍 UP Wheat Zones

  • Doab Regions: Ganga-Yamuna Doab (Meerut, Aligarh, Etah)
  • Rohilkhand: Bareilly, Rampur, Moradabad
  • Agra Division: Agra, Mathura, Firozabad
  • Least in Poorvanchal: Eastern UP prefers rice

🌡️ Geographical Requirements

  • Cool dry winters (15–20°C) at sowing time (Oct–Nov)
  • Warm summers at harvesting (March–April)
  • Sandy loam soils — well-drained alluvial
  • Rainfall 50–75 cm OR irrigation
  • Rabi crop — grown Oct to March
🧠 Mnemonic (Wheat States): "PUMP-R" = Punjab, UP, MP, Pajasthan (Raj), RHaryana → All major wheat producers

Rice Belt — East India + UP Poorvanchal

EXAM HOT

🌿 Core Rice States

  • West Bengal — Bardhaman (Rice Bowl of India); 3 crops/year
  • Assam — Brahmaputra Valley (Bodo areas)
  • Odisha — Coastal plains, Cuttack, Puri
  • Andhra Pradesh + Telangana — Krishna, Godavari delta
  • Tamil Nadu — Kaveri delta (Thanjavur = "Granary of South")
  • Kerala — Kuttanad (below sea level rice farming)

📍 UP Rice Zones (Poorvanchal)

  • Varanasi Division: Varanasi, Chandauli, Ghazipur
  • Gorakhpur Division: Gorakhpur, Deoria, Kushinagar
  • Azamgarh Division: Azamgarh, Mau, Ballia
  • Faizabad Division: Ayodhya, Ambedkar Nagar
  • Kharif crop; heavy monsoon rainfall 100–200 cm

🌾 Special Rice Varieties (UPPSC)

  • GI Tag Rice: Govind Bhog (Bengal), Kalanamak (UP's Siddharth Nagar)
  • Kalanamak: Ancient Buddhist-era rice, UP specialty
  • Basmati: Dehradun, Saharanpur, Bijnor (UP) + Haryana + Punjab
  • Thanjavur = "Rice Granary of South India"
🧠 Mnemonic (Rice Belt): "BOAT-WO" = Bengal, Odisha, Assam, TN, WB+Telangana, Orientation East → Rice grows in Eastern India + Kerala

Cotton Belt — Maharashtra + Gujarat

🌱 Deccan Cotton Triangle

  • Maharashtra: Vidarbha (Nagpur, Wardha, Yavatmal) — Black Cotton Soil (Regur)
  • Gujarat: Saurashtra (Rajkot, Surendranagar, Bhavnagar)
  • Telangana: Adilabad, Nizamabad, Warangal
  • Punjab + Haryana: Long-staple American cotton
  • Rajasthan: Sri Ganganagar (canal-fed cotton)

🏭 Cotton + Textile Link

  • Black soil (Regur) = best for cotton; retains moisture
  • Maharashtra: Largest cotton producer by area
  • Gujarat: Highest productivity (BT cotton revolution)
  • Manchester of India = Ahmedabad (historically)
  • Manchester of East = Osaka (Japan), NOT Indian
🧠 Mnemonic: "Black soil → Cotton" = "विदर्भ में काली मिट्टी, कपास की खेती" — Regur soil = regulates moisture = cotton thrives

Tea Belts — Assam, Darjeeling, Nilgiri

🍵 Assam Tea

  • Location: Brahmaputra Valley + Barak Valley
  • Districts: Dibrugarh, Jorhat, Sibsagar, Tinsukia, Cachar
  • Largest tea producing state in India (~55%)
  • Flat plains, alluvial soil; heavy rainfall 200–300 cm
  • CTC tea (Crush, Tear, Curl) = Assam specialty

🏔️ Darjeeling Tea

  • Location: Darjeeling Hills, West Bengal (North)
  • GI Tag: First Indian product to get GI Tag (2004)
  • Altitude 600–2000m; cool climate; muscatel flavor
  • Districts: Darjeeling, Kurseong, Kalimpong
  • Called "Champagne of Teas"

🌿 Nilgiri Tea

  • Location: Nilgiri Hills, Tamil Nadu
  • Districts: Ooty (Nilgiris), Coonoor, Kotagiri
  • Also: Munnar (Kerala Wayanad district)
  • Year-round production due to tropical climate
  • 3rd largest tea zone after Assam + Darjeeling

☕ Other Tea Regions

  • Kangra Valley: Himachal Pradesh (oldest tea estate)
  • Dooars + Terai: WB plains (foothills zone)
  • Palampur: Himachal Pradesh
  • India = 2nd largest tea producer (China = 1st)
  • India = Largest tea exporter historically
🧠 Mnemonic (Tea 3 zones): "AND" = Assam + Nilgiri + Darjeeling → A-N-D… AND all produce tea!

Nuclear Power Plants (NPCIL)

7 OPERATIONAL SITES

☢️ Nuclear Tarapur, Maharashtra

  • India's 1st Nuclear Plant (1969)
  • Capacity: 1400 MW (TAPS 1-4)
  • Palghar district, Maharashtra
  • US collaboration (GE)

☢️ Nuclear Rawatbhata, Rajasthan

  • Rajasthan Atomic Power Project (RAPP)
  • Chittorgarh district, Rajasthan
  • Capacity: 1180 MW
  • Canadian collaboration initially

☢️ Nuclear Kudankulam, Tamil Nadu

  • Tirunelveli district, Tamil Nadu
  • Russia (Rosatom) collaboration
  • Largest nuclear plant (2000 MW operational)
  • 2 more units under construction

☢️ Nuclear Kaiga, Karnataka

  • Uttara Kannada district, Karnataka
  • Capacity: 880 MW
  • Western Ghats forest area
  • World record for continuous reactor operation

☢️ Nuclear Kakrapar, Gujarat

  • Surat district, Gujarat
  • Tapi River bank
  • Capacity: 440+700 MW (units 3&4 added)
  • PHWR type reactor

☢️ Nuclear Narora, UP

  • Bulandshahr district, UP
  • Ganga river bank
  • Capacity: 440 MW
  • UP's only nuclear plant!

☢️ Nuclear Kalpakkam, Tamil Nadu

  • Chengalpattu district, TN (near Chennai)
  • MAPS (Madras APS) + Fast Breeder Reactor
  • India's 1st Fast Breeder Test Reactor
  • Capacity: 440 MW + under construction
🧠 Nuclear Plants Mnemonic: "T-R-K-K-K-N-K" = Tarapur(MH) + Rawatbhata(RJ) + Kudankulam(TN) + Kaiga(KA) + Kakrapar(GJ) + Narora(UP) + Kalpakkam(TN) → "Try Reaching Kudankulam, Kaiga, Kakrapar, Narora, Kalpakkam!"

Questions Answered
1Which of the following is called the "Granary of South India"?
(a) Krishna Delta (AP)
(b) Thanjavur (Tamil Nadu)
(c) Bardhaman (West Bengal)
(d) Kuttanad (Kerala)
✅ Thanjavur (TN) is the "Granary of South India" due to Kaveri delta rice cultivation. Bardhaman (WB) = Rice Bowl of India. Kuttanad = below sea level farming.
2India's largest container port is —
(a) Mumbai Port
(b) Kandla Port
(c) JNPT (Nhava Sheva), Maharashtra
(d) Chennai Port
✅ JNPT (Jawaharlal Nehru Port Trust) in Raigad district, Maharashtra handles ~50% of India's container traffic. Kandla handles maximum total cargo tonnage.
3UP's only Nuclear Power Plant is located at —
(a) Narora, Bulandshahr
(b) Kakrapar, Sonbhadra
(c) Tarapur, Palghar
(d) Rawatbhata, Agra
✅ Narora Atomic Power Station (NAPS) is in Bulandshahr district, UP on the Ganga river bank. Capacity: 440 MW. Kakrapar is in Gujarat (Surat), Tarapur in Maharashtra, Rawatbhata in Rajasthan.
4Which railway zone has its headquarters at Prayagraj?
(a) Northern Railway
(b) North Eastern Railway
(c) East Central Railway
(d) North Central Railway
✅ North Central Railway (NCR) has HQ at Prayagraj. Northern Railway = New Delhi. North Eastern Railway = Gorakhpur. East Central Railway = Hajipur.
5India's longest dam (Hirakud Dam) is built on which river?
(a) Godavari River
(b) Mahanadi River
(c) Damodar River
